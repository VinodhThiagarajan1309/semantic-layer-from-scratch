Corrections log

What a reader flagged while working through the build, and what changed.

This book is read by a real person. Vinodh reads it chapter by chapter and does every build step himself in Stardog Studio and DBeaver. Whenever a passage confuses him, or Studio shows something different from the page, he says so, and the passage is fixed in place in the same session. This page records those fixes.

Each entry gives the date, the part of the book, what the reader flagged, and what changed. Entries are grouped by chapter in reading order, newest first within each chapter. Small typo and wording fixes are not listed one by one.

56 corrections so far, from September 23 to October 1, 2026. Chapters after 10 have not been read closely yet, so their entries will come later.
