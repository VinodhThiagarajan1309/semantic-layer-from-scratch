The Semantic Layer, From Scratch

Building a federated knowledge graph over Aurora and Redshift with Stardog, and putting an AI agent on top

Vinodh Thiagarajan

About this book. Fifteen short chapters that take a database engineer from "what is a triple?" to a working AI agent answering questions live across Aurora PostgreSQL and Amazon Redshift through Stardog. Each chapter is a self-contained page with hands-on steps in Stardog Studio. Inspired by the AWS Machine Learning blog post on building a semantic layer with Stardog and Amazon Bedrock AgentCore.

Who this is for

You are an engineer who knows databases very well: SQL, joins, schemas, Postgres, Redshift, ETL. You have never worked with RDF, triples, SPARQL, OWL, ontologies, reasoning rules, SHACL, Stardog, Voicebox or MCP, and you would like to understand them properly rather than copy a demo. This book takes you from "what is a triple?" to a working AI agent that answers business questions live across two AWS databases, explaining every new idea in terms of the SQL you already know and saying plainly where the comparison breaks.

How to read it

  • Keep Stardog Studio open beside the book, connected to the database kit-c360. Keep DBeaver handy to check answers in SQL.
  • Do every green "Try it in Studio" box yourself. Practice steps use scratch graphs and clean up after themselves; chapters marked build step leave something permanent that later chapters rely on, so do them in order.
  • Ask Claude whenever something is unclear. After each build step, press its "Build step N done" button and paste it to Claude with your results, so we can confirm them before the next step builds on them.
  • Code blocks have a Copy button. Pages follow your system's light or dark setting.

Contents

Time to finish: about 10 hours in total: roughly 4.3 hours of reading and 5.8 hours hands-on in Studio and DBeaver. One or two chapters per sitting works well. The build chapters take longer because you create something real.
